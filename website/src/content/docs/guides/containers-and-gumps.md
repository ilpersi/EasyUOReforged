Screens of the client and the values the `#Cont…` variables show for them, with the positions of their buttons
for [`click`](/commands/click/). Positions are in pixels inside the client window.

:::caution[Collected for clients of around 2006]
These screens, their sizes and button positions come from the 640×480 login screens of clients of that time.
Newer clients look different: check the values on your own client with the variable inspector before relying on
them. The original table labelled the gump codes `#ContType`; they are [`#ContKind`](/variables/contkind/) values.
:::
