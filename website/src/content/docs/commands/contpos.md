---
title: "ContPos"
description: "Moves the active topmost gump to a new position."
kind: command
category: client
syntax: "contPos {X-Coordinate} {Y-Coordinate}"
needsClient: true
source: wiki
---

The contPos command moves the currently active topmost gump to the coordinates specified.

## Example

```euo
msg bank $
wait 20
contpos 10 10
halt
```
