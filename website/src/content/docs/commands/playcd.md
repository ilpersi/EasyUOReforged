`PlayCD` is a leftover from early EasyUO versions. EasyUO 1.5 still recognised the word but did nothing
with it, and EasyUO Reforged keeps it that way: a line starting with `PlayCD` is accepted and does nothing,
whatever follows it. Remove it from your scripts.
